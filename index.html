About

Hi, I'm Bolaji

I'm the founder of Bijay Media, a small digital studio that helps store owners, small businesses and founders sell and operate online.

I've worked in e-commerce since 2019, starting in dropshipping, and I've delivered client projects on Fiverr since 2022. That background means I look at product pages, checkout and customer questions the way a store owner does, not only the way a developer does.

Alongside client work I'm studying Mechanical Engineering at Olusegun Agagu University of Science and Technology, which taught me to break messy problems down into systems.
